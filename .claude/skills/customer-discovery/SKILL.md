# customer discovery

Each area below names when to use it and points to its full guidance under
`references/`.

## Value first, always

Every interaction — an email, a call, a file, a follow-up — starts from one
specific person: what is on their plate right now, and what would be genuinely
useful to them this week. Give that first; ask your question second. Work it
out per person, with a source, before anything is sent or scheduled. If you
cannot say what is useful to them now, you are not ready to contact them.
